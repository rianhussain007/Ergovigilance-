# ErgoVigilance — Paid Ergonomics Assessment
### A scored, evidence-based posture-risk assessment for a single line or station

## What's included
- 2–3 days of camera-based data capture on the station(s) you choose — temporary setup, no permanent install, no IT integration required
- RULA/REBA-informed risk scoring per worker and task, from the same pipeline behind the ErgoVigilance platform
- A PDF report: risk distribution, per-task breakdown, specific recommendations
- A short readout call to walk through the findings

## Why this instead of a subscription
Much lower commitment than an ongoing monitoring deployment — no camera stays installed, no long-term contract. It's a fixed-scope engagement, priced like consulting, not sold like software.

## Suggested pricing — validate before quoting

Indian ergonomics-audit engagements broadly run in the ₹50,000–₹3,00,000 range depending on scope and provider. That figure comes from office-ergonomics consulting, not factory-floor-specific work, so treat it as a ceiling reference, not a target — you're newer and unproven, which should show up in the price.

| Package | Suggested price | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| First 1–2 customers | Free, or a nominal ₹5,000–10,000 | In exchange for a written testimonial + permission to use the (anonymized) data for validation |
| Single-station assessment | ₹15,000–₹25,000 | One line/station, 2–3 days, one PDF report |
| Multi-station assessment | ₹40,000–₹75,000 | Up to 4 stations, comparative report across them |

These are starting anchors, not a researched number for this exact service — get 3–5 real conversations before you lock in a rate card. Price low enough that "no" is rare for the first few customers; the field data and case study you get back are worth more than the fee right now.

## Natural next step
Every paid assessment doubles as a pilot audition. If the findings land well, offer to leave a camera running for two weeks at no extra cost, on the same terms as the platform pilot — that's how this converts into the recurring product.

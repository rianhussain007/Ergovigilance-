# ErgoVigilance
### AI-assisted ergonomic risk screening — from an ordinary camera

**Continuous posture-risk screening from an ordinary camera, with instant alerts, coaching, and audit-grade reports.**

## The problem
Manual RULA/REBA ergonomics audits are point-in-time, observer-dependent, and paper-based. They can't be compared across stations or shifts, and they miss almost everything that happens between visits.

## What it does
A standard camera feeds a pose-estimation pipeline that scores posture against RULA/REBA biomechanical criteria in real time, flags sustained risk, and gives workers and supervisors plain-language guidance. No wearables.

- **On-premise (free):** runs on a laptop with a USB webcam, rule-based scoring, video never leaves the building.
- **Cloud:** scales to CCTV/RTSP across many cameras with a central dashboard, for sites ready to move past a single station.

## Built for four roles
Operators see their own posture and tips, nothing else. Supervisors see live risk across stations. Safety/EHS managers get trend reports, audit trails, and evidence exports. Admins manage users, cameras, and retention.

## What we can say today
- **87.6% agreement with human assessors**, measured on 500 labeled frames from controlled testing (LOW/MEDIUM risk categories; HIGH-risk accuracy not yet independently validated; single-site data so far — each pilot expands this).
- Ready for **controlled pilot evaluation**: one site, one to two cameras, consented and supervised.

**Honest positioning:** AI-assisted ergonomic risk screening and decision-support tool. **Not** a medical device. **Not** clinically validated. **Not yet** enterprise-qualified — we'll tell you that directly, because an inflated claim in a safety product is a liability for both of us.

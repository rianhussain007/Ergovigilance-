# ErgoVigilance — What This Is, For You

*A one-page explanation for the person whose workstation is being monitored.
Give this to them BEFORE the camera is set up. Keep it plain and honest.*

---

## What is happening?

For the next 2 weeks, a camera at your workstation will watch how you work so
we can measure **physical strain** — awkward neck/back/shoulder positions,
how long they last, and how often they happen. This is an **ergonomics
assessment**, the same kind a consultant would do by standing and watching,
except it runs continuously instead of for 15 minutes.

## What is NOT happening

- ❌ Nobody is watching you live like security footage. The video is processed
  by software on a computer **right here in the building**; no one sits and
  watches you.
- ❌ The video is **never uploaded to the internet or any cloud service.** It
  stays on the machine in your building.
- ❌ It is **not a medical device** and does not diagnose injuries or health
  conditions.
- ❌ It does **not** measure how fast you work, whether you take breaks, or
  anything about your performance. Only posture strain.

## What happens to the video?

- It is processed into **numbers** (joint angles, posture risk scores).
- The raw video is stored only for the assessment and **automatically deleted
  after 30 days** (or sooner on request).
- Only people responsible for safety at your site — plus the assessment team —
  can view the results. **Your manager cannot use this to discipline you.** If
  you ever feel it is being used that way, say so — that is a violation of how
  this system is meant to work.

## What do we do with the results?

We look for **patterns** — for example "workers at this station spend a lot of
time with their neck bent." The goal is to find ways to make the work less
physically demanding: better equipment, different task rotation, adjusted
workstation height. **The purpose is to protect you, not to watch you.**

## Your rights

- **You can decline.** You are not required to participate. If you decline,
  the camera will not be pointed at your workstation.
- **You can ask questions any time.** Ask your supervisor, or contact:
  `________________________________` (name / phone / email).
- **You can ask for your data to be deleted** at any point during or after the
  pilot. This is a legal right (right to erasure) and will be honored.

## Important limitation — please read

The system uses **heuristic thresholds and is not clinically validated**. Its
risk scores are an *early-warning screening aid* for prioritizing where to look
closer — they are **not** a medical diagnosis, and they **cannot prove** that a
specific job or movement caused an injury. If you are experiencing pain or
discomfort at work, **report it through your normal safety process** and see a
qualified professional — do not rely on this system, and do not ignore your own
body because a screen says something.

## In one sentence

*A camera watches your workstation for 2 weeks so we can find ways to make the
work easier on your body. The video stays in the building, is deleted after 30
days, and can never be used against you.*

## The problem

Musculoskeletal injuries are the most common workplace injury in manufacturing —
and most plants find out about them from a workers' compensation claim, weeks
after the damage is done. Manual observation is too slow to catch the pattern,
and paper logs can't tell you *which* motion is the problem.

## The solution — one sentence

**ErgoVigilance watches your workers' posture in real time from an ordinary
camera, flags risky motion the moment it happens, and leaves you with
evidence-backed reports — with the video never leaving your building.**

## What it does (all working today)

| Capability | What it means on your floor |
|---|---|
| **Real-time posture monitoring** | Every worker at a station gets a live risk score (OK / Watch your back / STOP) |
| **Per-person tracking** | Camera detects every person, identifies each by face (with consent) or badge/QR — or stays anonymous |
| **Anti-spoof liveness** | A photo or screen held to the camera is detected and flagged — never counted as a person |
| **Instant alerts** | Unsafe posture triggers an alert + on-screen warning in seconds |
| **Reports that hold up** | Per-session PDF/CSV/JSON reports, plus a one-click evidence package for OSHA/insurance review |
| **Completely offline** | Runs on a laptop or local server. No cloud. Video never leaves the plant. Works on a local Wi-Fi/hotspot |
| **Benchmark comparison** | "Your line's neck-flexion is in the Xth percentile of the N sessions on record" |

## The risk-free offer

- **$0 for 2 weeks.** We install it in ~30 minutes on a laptop or station PC.
- **No IT integration required.** Standalone — no Active Directory, no cloud account.
- **No lock-in.** If you don't see value, we remove it ourselves. Nothing to uninstall.

## What it does NOT do (we're explicit)

- It is **not a medical device** — it is an early-warning screening tool.
- It does **not** send any video or data to the cloud. Ever.
- It does **not** identify anyone without consent — badge-only and anonymous modes work the same.

## The pilot conversation

1. **You pick one station** where posture matters (assembly, packing, inspection).
2. **We point the camera** and walk through the 4-step setup check (position, lighting, framing, face check).
3. **You watch for 2 weeks** — alerts, reports, benchmark.
4. **You decide.** The reports alone usually justify keeping it.

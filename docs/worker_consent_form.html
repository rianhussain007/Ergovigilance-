Worker Consent & Acknowledgment Form

Purpose: This form explains how ErgoVigilance monitors workplace ergonomics to protect your health and safety. Your consent is required before the system can analyze your posture data.

1. What is ErgoVigilance?

ErgoVigilance is an AI-powered ergonomic monitoring system that:

  • Analyzes your body posture in real-time using camera-based pose detection
  • Calculates ergonomic risk scores based on international standards (RULA/REBA)
  • Provides immediate feedback when your posture indicates injury risk
  • Generates reports to help management improve workplace conditions

2. What Data is Collected?

Data Type Description Retention
Body Pose keypoints 17-33 digital landmarks on your body (shoulders, elbows, hips, knees) 30 days
Risk Scores Ergonomic risk level (Low/Medium/High) calculated from your posture 1 year
Task Classification Detected activity (lifting, reaching, assembly, etc.) 1 year
Session Metadata Duration, timestamp, camera ID 1 year
⚠️ What is NOT collected:
  • Facial recognition or identity verification
  • Video recordings (only pose data is stored)
  • Personal conversations or audio
  • Location tracking outside the workstation

3. How Your Data is Used

Safety Monitoring: Real-time alerts when your posture indicates injury risk
Ergonomic Improvement: Aggregated data helps management redesign workstations
Training: anonymized data used for ergonomic training programs

4. Your Rights

  • Right to Know: You can ask what data has been collected about you
  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of your posture data
  • Right to Correct: You can request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Deletion: You can request deletion of your data (subject to safety requirements)
  • Right to Opt-Out: You may decline consent at any time (see Section 6)

5. Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • End-to-end encryption during transmission
  • Encrypted storage on secure servers
  • Role-based access control (only authorized safety personnel)
  • Regular security audits and compliance checks

🔒 Your data is used ONLY for safety — never for performance evaluation

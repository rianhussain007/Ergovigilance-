# Service Level Terms — Cloud Professional / Enterprise (DRAFT)

> **Status: DRAFT for sign-off + lawyer review (see SELL_READINESS_AUDIT
> Q5). Do not attach to any quote or contract until both are recorded.**

## Scope

Applies to paid Cloud Professional and Enterprise deployments only.
Self-hosted Starter, free pilots, and paid assessments are best-effort
and carry no response targets.

## Response targets (response, not resolution)

| Severity | Definition | Target response |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | System down: no alerts, no dashboard, no reports | 4 business hours |
| Standard | Degraded feature, wrong report, billing question | Next business day |

"Business hours" and escalation contacts are agreed per contract.
Targets are goals, not guarantees — see Credits.

## Uptime

The 99.5% figure on the Pricing page is a **target**, consistent with the
page wording. No uptime guarantee is offered until a production track
record exists (see TRL-9 lean bar: 3-month uptime log).

## Exclusions

Site network/power outages, camera hardware failures, customer
infrastructure changes, force majeure, and any self-hosted deployment.
Support does not cover ergonomics consulting — the product is a
screening aid, not a certified audit (see Safe Claims Sheet).

## Credits

No service credits during pilot-phase contracts. Credit terms, if any,
are negotiated per Enterprise contract — never promised in sales copy.

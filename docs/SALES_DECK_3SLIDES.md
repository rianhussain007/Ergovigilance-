## Slide 1 — The Problem

**Title:** "Musculoskeletal injuries are the #1 cost you don't see coming"

- Workers' comp claims arrive weeks after the injury — by then the pattern is invisible.
- Manual observation doesn't scale to every station, every shift.
- Paper logs can't answer: *which motion, which worker, how often?*

**Script (45s):**
> "Every plant we've talked to finds out about posture injuries from a claim,
> not from prevention. By the time a worker files, the damage is done — and
> nobody can say which motion or which station caused it. That's the gap
> ErgoVigilance closes: it watches continuously so you catch the pattern in
> week one, not week twelve."

**Visual:** the Dashboard screenshot showing live risk + alerts.

## Slide 2 — The Solution

**Title:** "Real-time posture monitoring, 100% offline"

- One ordinary camera → every worker at the station gets a live risk score.
- Unsafe posture → instant on-screen alert + report you can hand to anyone.
- **No cloud. No video leaves the building.** Runs on a laptop or local server.
- Identify by face (with consent), by badge/QR, or not at all — privacy is a choice, not a compromise.

**Script (60s):**
> "We point a camera at one station. ErgoVigilance detects every person,
> scores each one's posture in real time, and the moment something is unsafe
> you get an alert — plus a report with the evidence. Two things competitors
> can't say: the video never leaves your building, and if a worker doesn't
> want to be identified, they don't have to be — the posture monitoring works
> exactly the same."
